# Privacy Policy

**Tornado Gallery**

_Last updated: 19 July 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how **Flockit** ("we," "us," or "our") collects,
uses, and shares information about you when you use the Tornado Gallery mobile
application (the "Services").

## 1. Introduction

Tornado Gallery is designed to operate primarily in **local mode**. The privacy
of your data is our absolute priority. Since the app does not require the
creation of an account and does not upload your content to external servers,
control of your data remains entirely in your hands.

## 2. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect only the information strictly necessary for the operation of the
application.

### Information You Voluntarily Provide

- **Communications:** If you contact us directly for technical support, we may
  receive your email address and the content of your message.

### Application Data and Permissions

To function properly, the app requires access to certain device features:

- **Gallery and Storage:** Required to select images for encryption and to save
  encrypted/decrypted images in the destination folders.
- **Camera:** If you choose to take a photo directly from the app to instantly
  encrypt it.
- **File Access:** Required for automatic deletion of the original file (if
  selected by the user).

**Please note:** We do not collect or transmit to our servers the original
images, encrypted images, or passphrases used for encryption. The entire process
takes place **locally on your device**.

## 3. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the app's features.
- Manage the encryption and decryption operations requested by the user.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and bugs.
- Ensure the security of the application.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so,
namely:

- **Consent:** When you explicitly grant us permission to access your gallery or
  camera through system permissions.
- **Performance of a Contract:** To provide you with the encryption service
  requested through use of the app.
- **Legitimate Interests:** To improve the stability of the application.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. The only exception
concerns compliance with legal obligations or the protection of our rights in the
event of legal disputes, although, given that we hold no data about your files on
our servers, our ability to provide any such information is practically nil.

## 6. In-App Purchases

If you purchase **Tornado Gallery Pro**, the transaction is handled **entirely by
the Apple App Store or Google Play**. We do not receive or store your name,
payment card, or billing details — the app only learns whether a valid Pro
entitlement is active. The processing of your purchase is subject to the privacy
policy of the applicable store:

- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 7. Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Images and passphrases remain on your device. If you uninstall
  the app or clear the app's data, locally stored information (excluding photos
  saved to the phone's public storage) may be lost.
- **Communications:** We retain support emails only for as long as necessary to
  resolve the request.

## 8. Data Security

We use advanced encryption protocols to protect your images. However, the
security of the passphrase depends entirely on the user. Please be aware that:

- If you lose the encryption passphrase, we will be unable to recover your
  images.
- No electronic storage system is 100% secure, but Tornado Gallery's "offline"
  approach drastically reduces the risk of remote attacks.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic location (e.g., GDPR in Europe), you have the right
to:

- Access the data we hold about you (primarily your support communications).
- Request the deletion or rectification of your data.
- Revoke gallery or camera access permissions from your device settings at any
  time.

## 10. Minors

Our Services are not directed to persons under the age of 13 (or 16, depending on
the jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover
that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will promptly delete it.

## 11. Do-Not-Track Controls

Since our app does not track web browsing activities and operates locally,
Do-Not-Track (DNT) signals do not have a direct impact on the use of the
application.
