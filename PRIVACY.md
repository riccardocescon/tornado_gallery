# Privacy Policy

**Tornado Gallery**

_Last updated: 19 July 2026_

Your privacy is the reason Tornado Gallery exists. This Privacy Policy explains
what data the App handles and — more importantly — what it does **not** collect.

## 1. Summary

Tornado Gallery is a fully **offline** application. **We do not collect, store,
transmit, or have access to any of your personal data, images, passwords, or
encryption keys.** There are no servers, no accounts, and no backend. Everything
stays on your device.

## 2. No Accounts, No Servers, No Backend

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. It does not connect
to any server operated by us. All processing — selecting images, encrypting,
decrypting, and storing them — happens **entirely on your device**. Your images
and passwords **never leave your device** and are never sent to us or to any
third party.

## 3. Data We Collect

**None.** We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, device identifiers,
advertising identifiers, location data, or any other personal information. The
App contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

## 4. Images and Encryption Keys

- Images you select and encrypt are stored **locally** in the App's storage on
  your device.
- Encryption is performed on-device using AES-256. Encryption keys are derived
  from the password you choose and are **never stored or transmitted** in a form
  we can access.
- **We cannot recover your images or passwords.** If you forget a password, the
  corresponding data cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us.

## 5. Device Permissions

The App requests access to your device's **photos / gallery** solely to let you
select images to encrypt and to save decrypted images back when you choose to.
This access is used only on your device and only for these purposes. The App does
not upload, index, or transmit your photos.

## 6. Purchases

If you purchase **Tornado Gallery Pro**, the transaction is handled **entirely by
the Apple App Store or Google Play**. We do not receive or store your name,
payment card, or billing details — the App only learns whether a valid Pro
entitlement is active. The processing of your purchase is subject to the privacy
policy of the applicable store:

- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you remain in full control of it. You can
delete encrypted images from within the App at any time. **Uninstalling the App
removes its local data from your device.** We hold no copy of your data and
therefore have nothing to delete on our side.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children. As the App collects no data, it poses no data-collection risk
to users of any age.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be
indicated by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after
changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
